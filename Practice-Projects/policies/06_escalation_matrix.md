# Arrowline Logistics — Escalation Matrix
*Document ID: ARW-POL-006 · Version 1.8*

## 1. Customer tiers

| Tier | Escalation SLA | First response | Account manager involved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold | 24 hours | 2 hours | Immediately |
| Silver | 48 hours | 4 hours | On second contact |
| Bronze | 72 hours | 8 hours | On written complaint |

## 2. Who handles what

| Situation | Owner |
|---|---|
| Shipment in transit, within promise | Support agent (automated reply acceptable) |
| Delay up to 48 hours | Support agent, with hub status check |
| Delay beyond 48 hours | Destination hub manager + account manager |
| `LOSS` reported | Claims team, always a named human |
| Any claim above ₹25,000 | Claims manager |
| Suspected undeclared or prohibited goods | Compliance team — never handled by support |
| Customer threatens legal action or regulatory complaint | Account manager + legal, same day |

## 3. What a support agent must never do

- Promise a settlement amount, a settlement date, or an outcome before adjudication.
- Confirm or deny liability on a `LOSS` or `DAMAGE` claim.
- Share another customer's shipment, claim or contact information.
- Waive the claim window, the declaration requirement, or any mandatory rejection reason.
