# Arrowline Logistics — Packaging Guidelines
*Document ID: ARW-POL-003 · Version 2.4 · Effective 1 January 2026*

Packaging is the shipper's responsibility. Damage claims are assessed against the minimum
packaging standard for the contents category.

| Contents category | Minimum acceptable packaging |
|---|---|
| Electronics | `Carton-Double` or `Crate` |
| Lithium Batteries | `Crate` (and see ARW-POL-004) |
| Furniture | `Crate` or `Pallet` |
| Auto Parts | `Carton-Double`, `Crate` or `Pallet` |
| Pharma (non-cold) | `Carton-Single` or better |
| FMCG | `Carton-Single` or better |
| Apparel | `Carton-Single` or better |
| Documents | `Envelope` or better |

`Pallet` and `Crate` are considered equivalent or superior to `Carton-Double` for all
categories except Electronics, where a `Pallet` alone is **not** acceptable because individual
cartons remain exposed.

A `DAMAGE` claim where the recorded packaging falls below the minimum for the category is
rejected with `REJECTED_PACKAGING`. Packaging has no bearing on `LOSS` or `SLA_BREACH` claims.
