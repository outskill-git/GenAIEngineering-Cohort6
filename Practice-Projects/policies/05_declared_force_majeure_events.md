# Arrowline Logistics — Declared Force Majeure Events (Register)
*Document ID: ARW-POL-005 · Maintained by Network Operations · Updated monthly*

Only events listed in this register are force majeure for the purposes of ARW-POL-001 §4 and
ARW-POL-002 §4. Operational congestion, vehicle breakdown, staff shortage and ordinary
seasonal weather are **not** force majeure.

| Event ID | Description | Hubs affected | From | To |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FM-2026-01 | Cyclone Mithili landfall and port closure | MAA, COK | 2026-05-02 | 2026-05-09 |
| FM-2026-02 | State-wide transporters' strike | JAI, AMD | 2026-07-23 | 2026-07-26 |
| FM-2026-03 | Chennai urban flooding | MAA | 2026-11-12 | 2026-11-20 |

**Note from Network Operations (September 2026):** the hub congestion experienced at Nagpur
(NAG) during August 2026 was caused by a backlog in outbound trucking capacity. A request to
classify it as force majeure was **reviewed and declined**; it is therefore an operational
failure and claims arising from it are payable in the normal way.
